# Ticker Rules

## How to Play

Ticker is a fantasy Premier League game where you choose clubs, not players.

Build a portfolio of four Premier League clubs, earn points from their real-world performances, and trade clubs as their market values rise and fall throughout the season.

Your goal is simple:

> **Score the most fantasy points while building the smartest football portfolio.**

---

## 1. Selecting Your Initial Portfolio

### Starting Budget

Every manager begins with **$100.00** in available cash.

Use that budget to purchase exactly **four Premier League clubs** before the first Gameweek deadline.

The combined purchase price of your four clubs may not exceed your available cash.

#### Example

| Club | Opening Price |
|---|---:|
| Arsenal | $30.00 |
| Aston Villa | $25.00 |
| Brighton | $23.00 |
| Bournemouth | $18.00 |
| **Total Invested** | **$96.00** |
| **Cash Remaining** | **$4.00** |

Any money not spent remains in your account and may be used for future trades.

### Portfolio Rules

Your initial portfolio must contain:

- Exactly four different Premier League clubs
- No duplicate clubs
- A total purchase price of $100.00 or less

The same club may be owned by multiple managers. Purchasing a club does not remove it from the market or prevent another manager from owning it.

### Club Prices

Every Premier League club is assigned an opening market value before the season begins.

Opening prices may reflect factors such as:

- Expected performance
- Schedule strength
- Recent form
- Market expectations

After the season begins, club values may rise or fall based on real-world performance and other approved market factors.

A club's current value may therefore be higher or lower than the price you originally paid.

### Completing Your Initial Portfolio

You may make unlimited changes to your initial portfolio before the first Gameweek deadline.

Once the deadline passes, those four clubs are locked in as your first Gameweek Scoring Lineup — but the market itself never closes, so standard 24/7 trading rules apply from that point on.

---

## 2. Scoring

Ticker league standings are determined by **fantasy points**, not portfolio value.

Every club in your portfolio earns points based on its real Premier League results.

Portfolio value affects your future spending power, but it does not directly add points to your league score.

### Match Scoring

| Match Event | Points |
|---|---:|
| Win | +5 |
| Draw | +2 |
| Loss | 0 |
| Each goal scored | +1 |
| Clean sheet | +2 |

### Scoring Examples

#### Arsenal wins 3–0

| Scoring Event | Points |
|---|---:|
| Win | +5 |
| Three goals scored | +3 |
| Clean sheet | +2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

#### Chelsea draws 2–2

| Scoring Event | Points |
|---|---:|
| Draw | +2 |
| Two goals scored | +2 |
| **Total** | **4** |

#### Brighton loses 1–3

| Scoring Event | Points |
|---|---:|
| Loss | 0 |
| One goal scored | +1 |
| **Total** | **1** |

### Gameweek Score

Your Gameweek score is the combined total earned by the four clubs locked into your **Gameweek Scoring Lineup** — a snapshot taken at the deadline, not necessarily what you currently hold.

#### Example

| Club | Points |
|---|---:|
| Arsenal | 10 |
| Aston Villa | 7 |
| Brighton | 1 |
| Bournemouth | 4 |
| **Gameweek Total** | **22** |

Your points accumulate throughout the Premier League season.

The manager with the most total fantasy points at the end of the season wins the league.

### Multiple Matches in One Gameweek

When a club plays more than once during a Gameweek, points from every eligible match are added together.

When a club has no eligible match, it earns zero points for that Gameweek.

### Postponed and Abandoned Matches

A postponed match does not score until it is officially played.

If a match is abandoned, points remain pending until the Premier League confirms an official result or schedules the match to be replayed.

Ticker may adjust scores when an official result or match statistic is corrected after the final whistle.

## 3. Trading

After selecting your initial portfolio, you may buy and sell clubs through the Ticker market.

Trading allows you to:

- Sell clubs whose outlook has weakened
- Buy clubs you believe are undervalued
- Respond to schedule changes and team form
- Increase your portfolio's overall market value
- Reposition your portfolio for future Gameweeks

### The Ticker Market Is Open 24/7

> **Your lineup locks. Your portfolio never does.**

Unlike traditional fantasy transfer windows, the Ticker market stays open **24 hours a day, seven days a week** — before matches, during matches, even at halftime.

There is no limit to the number of clubs you may buy or sell, at any time, provided every transaction follows the portfolio and cash rules.

At each Gameweek deadline, Ticker takes an immutable snapshot of the four clubs you currently hold — this becomes that Gameweek's **Scoring Lineup**:

- Only these four clubs earn fantasy points for that Gameweek
- You must have owned exactly four different clubs at that moment
- Your cash balance may remain above $0.00

Trades submitted after the deadline update your **portfolio** and set up your **next** Scoring Lineup. They never change the Scoring Lineup that's already locked.

#### Example: selling a locked club during its match

You begin the Gameweek holding Arsenal, Chelsea, Brighton, and Aston Villa — all four lock in as your Scoring Lineup.

Chelsea falls behind 0-2 at halftime. You believe it will underperform and sell it immediately.

- **Fantasy impact:** none. Chelsea is still part of your locked Scoring Lineup, so whatever points it ultimately earns from that match still count toward your Gameweek score.
- **Financial impact:** immediate. Chelsea leaves your current portfolio at the price you sold it for, and you're free to spend the proceeds on another club right away — before the match, or Ticker's next price settlement, is even over.

### Buying Power

You may only purchase a club using your available cash.

Your available cash includes:

- Cash already held in your account
- Money received from selling a club

You may not borrow money, use margin, or allow your cash balance to become negative.

#### Example

You own a club currently worth **$30.00** and have **$5.00** already held in cash.

When you sell the club:

- Sale proceeds: $30.00
- Existing cash: $5.00
- New buying power: **$35.00**

You may purchase any club priced at **$35.00 or less**.

The replacement club is not limited to the value of the club you sold. It is limited by your **total available cash**.

### Selling a Club

When you sell a club, you receive its current market value, not its original purchase price.

#### Example

You purchase Arsenal for **$25.00**.

Arsenal's market value later rises to **$30.00**.

If you sell Arsenal, your account receives **$30.00** in cash. The additional $5.00 becomes realized portfolio growth and may be spent on another club.

If Arsenal's value instead falls to $22.00, selling it returns $22.00.

### Buying a Club

A purchase is completed at the club's displayed execution price.

Before approving a purchase, Ticker verifies that:

- You have enough available cash
- You do not already own the club
- The club's market is open
- The transaction follows all portfolio rules

The price shown on the final confirmation screen is the price used to complete the trade.

### Portfolio Requirements

A manager may temporarily own fewer than four clubs while completing trades during an open trading window.

However, the manager must own exactly four different clubs when the Gameweek deadline arrives.

A manager may never own more than four clubs at one time.

### Portfolio Value

Your total account value consists of:

> **Current value of owned clubs + available cash**

#### Example

| Asset | Value |
|---|---:|
| Liverpool | $30.00 |
| Aston Villa | $27.00 |
| Brighton | $25.00 |
| Bournemouth | $23.00 |
| Cash | $5.00 |
| **Total Portfolio Value** | **$110.00** |

Portfolio value measures the value of your football investments.

It does not replace fantasy points as the primary league score.

### Holding Cash

Unspent cash remains in your account until you use it.

Cash does not earn fantasy points or change in value, but keeping cash available may help you purchase a club later without selling as much of your portfolio.

### Match and Gameweek Locks

Ticker never closes the market just because football is being played. The only thing that locks each week is your **Scoring Lineup** — your portfolio, cash, and every future trade stay fully available at all times.

### When Points Transfer

The four clubs locked into your Scoring Lineup at the Gameweek deadline earn points for that Gameweek — regardless of what you do with your portfolio afterward.

#### Example

- You sell Arsenal before the Gameweek deadline.
- You buy Brighton before the Gameweek deadline.
- Arsenal's next eligible match does not count toward your score.
- Brighton's next eligible match does count toward your score.
- If instead you sell Arsenal **after** the deadline, while it's already locked into your Scoring Lineup, its points from that Gameweek still count — selling it only changes your portfolio and cash going forward.

Selling a club after it has already earned points for a completed Gameweek does not remove those points from your season total.

### Price Changes

Club prices may rise or fall during the season.

Price movements may reflect approved factors such as:

- Match results
- Goals scored
- Clean sheets
- Recent form
- Upcoming schedule
- Market expectations
- Buying and selling activity

Ticker determines all official club prices.

External betting odds, projections, or third-party valuations do not represent executable Ticker prices.

### No Direct Club Swaps

Trades are processed as two separate financial actions:

1. Sell a club and receive cash.
2. Use available cash to buy another club.

You are not directly exchanging one club for another.

Any money remaining after the new purchase stays in your cash balance.

---

## 4. Fantasy Points vs. Portfolio Value

Ticker includes two separate measures of success.

### Fantasy Points

Fantasy points determine your position in the league standings.

They are earned through the real-world performances of the clubs you own.

### Portfolio Value

Portfolio value determines your financial flexibility.

It rises or falls as the market value of your clubs changes and determines which clubs you can afford in future trades.

A manager may have the most valuable portfolio without having the most fantasy points, and vice versa.

> **Pick clubs that can score today without sacrificing the value you may need tomorrow.**
